about=A plugin for drawing Swedish comprehensive plans (oversiktsplaner) in QGIS following Boverket's regulations BFS 2024:2, the OP catalogue and GIS database model, and the Swedish Land Survey's national specification for comprehensive plans (test version). Creates a plan as a GeoPackage or in a shared PostGIS schema (with check-out and check-in), in SWEREF 99 TM or a local SWEREF 99 zone. Draw or import geometry and assign content from the OP catalogue, with web-safe symbology per content group. Plan details, decision and background documents (underlag) in one dialog. Links the plan document (Word) to the plan's content with bookmarks, with a web version (HTML). Exports to the Land Survey's JSON format with a check of what is missing. The user interface and documentation are in Swedish. Early development version.
